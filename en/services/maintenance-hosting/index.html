    Frequently asked questions about maintenance and hosting

    What does monthly maintenance include?

    CMS and plugin updates, backups, monitoring, small content changes and priority technical support. It starts from $55 USD/month (≈ $193,000 COP/month), without Colombian VAT.

    What is a VPS and what does this service include?

    A VPS is a virtual private server. This service includes deployment on a private server with NGINX, SSL, automatic backups and a staging environment, with no shared resources. It starts from $45 USD/month (≈ $158,000 COP/month).

    Who takes care of hosting in my projects?

    Except for the private VPS service, hosting is the client’s responsibility.

    Can you migrate my website to another server without taking it down?

    The migration service moves the website, database and settings to a new server with no downtime and no data loss (from $80 USD (≈ $280,000 COP)).

    How much does an hour of occasional support cost?

    Occasional hourly support costs $20 USD/hour (≈ $70,000 COP/hour), without Colombian VAT, and does not require a maintenance contract.
